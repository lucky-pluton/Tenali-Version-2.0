# Vanishing Buttons — Open Specification

## 1. Overview & Learning Objectives
* **Module ID:** `subtraction.vanishing-buttons`
* **FLN Code:** `FLN-NUM-SUB-ZERO`
* **NCERT Alignment:** Grade 1 Mathematics, Chapter 3 (*Vanishing Buttons & Discovery of Zero*)
* **Core Concept:** Subtraction as removal, decrementing by 1, and the ontological introduction of 0.

## 2. Pedagogical Framework
1. **Physical Story Narrative:** Gola the monkey wearing a shirt with buttons; eating bananas causes his tummy to expand and pop buttons.
2. **Stepwise Subtraction Ledger:** Records $4 - 1 = 3 \rightarrow 3 - 1 = 2 \rightarrow 2 - 1 = 1 \rightarrow 1 - 1 = 0$.
3. **The Zero Epiphany:** NCERT teachers' note emphasizes: *"Discuss the number of buttons left on the shirt at the end. Let children understand that zero is also a number like one, two, and other numbers."*

## 3. State Machine & Transitions
```
[Gola Shirt (N Buttons)] ➔ [Feed Banana] ➔ [Pop Button + Sound] ➔ [Decrement N] ➔ [If N===0: Zero Question] ➔ [Celebration Modal]
```

## 4. Level Progression
* **Level 1:** 4 buttons ($4 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 1 \rightarrow 0$).
* **Level 2:** 6 buttons ($6 \rightarrow 0$).
* **Level 3:** Subtraction Detective ($5 - ? = \text{Remaining}$).
