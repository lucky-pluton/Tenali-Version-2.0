# Marigold Magic – Decorate the School! 🇮🇳🌼

## Pedagogical Goal
Teaches early childhood (Ages 5–8 / Grades 1–2) counting, number recognition, and the core concept of addition as joining two discrete groups through an Indian Independence Day school decoration theme.

The progression is:
**COUNT INITIAL FLOWERS $\to$ ADD NEW FLOWERS $\to$ COUNT ALTOGETHER**

## Three-Stage Interactive Flow
1. **Stage 1 (Count Initial Garland):**
   - Teacher presents an orange marigold (*genda phool*) garland (*laddi*).
   - Children tap individual flowers to count them sequentially.
   - Pure count audio speaks: `"1", "2", "3"...` with gentle flower pluck sound.
   - Child answers: *"How many flowers are there in this laddi?"*

2. **Stage 2 (Add More Flowers!):**
   - Teacher opens her wicker basket and brings additional sunny yellow marigold flowers.
   - New flowers animate joining the garland one by one.
   - Original flowers remain vibrant orange; new flowers are sunny yellow.
   - Mathematical equation displayed: `origCount + addCount = ?`
   - Child counts the newly attached flowers or recounts all to determine the combined total.

3. **Stage 3 (Decorate and Celebrate!):**
   - The completed garland is hung in a celebratory scallop swag across the Indian Independence Day school wall with tricolour bunting.
   - Animated confetti, celebration fanfare, and joyful teacher/child responses.

## Accessibility & Child UX
- Every flower is distinct and individually tappable without clutter or overlapping.
- Pure counting speech (only numbers spoken on clicks).
- Story modal has mini-speaker for optional reading aloud and prominent Next button.
- Retries allowed without resetting progress.
